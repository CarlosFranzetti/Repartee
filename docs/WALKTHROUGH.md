# re:parteé: The Walkthrough

*A player's guide to the pixel-art point-and-click DJ adventure, from a crackling bedroom mixer to a festival main stage.*

> **Spoiler warning.** This guide gives away every puzzle and most of the jokes. For a nudge only, read the tips below.

---

## 1. The Premise

You are a broke bedroom DJ in New York City. You have three records, a mixer that sounds like bacon frying, a phone at
1%, and a neighbor who bangs on the pipe at eleven o'clock. Over five levels you hustle, dig, mix and talk your way
from Bushwick to a headline slot at the Harbor Lights festival.

| Level | Title | What you are trying to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bedroom DJ | Fix your gear, find a closer, and play your first gig at Nowhere on Saturday. |
| 2 | Underground Hustler | Raise cash, get turntables, save a dead PA, and win a monthly slot from Vic. |
| 3 | Club DJ | Win Thursday's anniversary night at the Halcyon Room without being robbed by Brandon Shine. |
| 4 | Touring DJ | Make a record, fly to Berlin with your bag intact, and play Halle Neun at 2 AM. |
| 5 | Superstar DJ | Build a rig, handle Axl Vanta, and headline Harbor Lights. |

## 2. Controls

- **Pick a verb, then click something.** The verbs are Look at, Pick up, Use, Open, Talk to, Push, Close, Give and Pull.
- **To Use or Give an item:** pick the verb, click the item in your inventory, then click the thing or person.
  A step in this guide such as "Use vodka swab on mixer" means exactly that.
- **Click the floor to walk.** Click a door or exit with Use to change rooms.
- **Right-click** (desktop) looks. **Space, Enter or a click** skips dialogue.
- **H or Tab** puts a label on every clickable thing in the room. Use it whenever you are stuck.
- **Keys:** L look, P pick up, U use, O open, T talk, G give, Esc to cancel a verb.
- **MENU** (or the phone) opens your messages, the subway map, your record crate, your setup, Save game and
  Load game (three slots). The map appears when you are free to travel. The game also saves as you go.
- **PAUSE** (or the Pause key, or Shift+P) freezes the game and the sound; it also pauses when you switch tabs.
  **SND** (or **M**) turns the sound off and on.
- **Mini-games** explain themselves on screen, and the title screen's Mini-Game Arcade replays them. Failing one never
  ruins the story: you can retry, and after enough misses the game moves you on.

## 3. Tips With No Spoilers

1. **Look at everything.** Every hotspot answers Look, and the jokes are half the game.
2. **Try the wrong verb.** Pushing, pulling or giving the wrong thing often gets a custom line.
3. **Talk to people twice.** Many characters have new things to say after you have done something.
4. **Listen to hints that sound like insults.** Ray, Lou and Odessa tell you what to do while pretending not to.
5. **Check the crate in your phone menu.** Every record has a real fact, and one level quizzes you on them.
6. **You cannot get stuck.** The game never lets you lose an item you still need.
7.

# Level 1: Bedroom DJ

> **Spoilers for Level 1 below.**

**Setup.** It is Tuesday night in Apartment 4C. Dante, who books Nowhere, has texted you, but you cannot call him on a
broken setup. You will fix the mixer, pass a phone audition, spend Wednesday earning money, stealing a USB stick and
digging for a fourth record, then play Nowhere on Saturday.

## Tuesday night in the bedroom

1. Pick up **under the bed**. You find the phone charger.
2. Use **phone** with **phone charger**. The phone wakes up and you read your texts.
3. Pick up the **pizza box**. There is a $5 bill stuck to the cheese.
4. Pick up the **empty cans**. You will cash them in later.
5. Use the **CDJs**. This is a quick deck test. Channel 2 crackles like bacon.
6. Open the **desk drawer**, then Pick up the **desk drawer** to take the cotton swabs and the vodka.
7. Use **cotton swabs** on **100-proof vodka**. You get a vodka swab.
8. Use **vodka swab** on **mixer**. The crackle is gone.
9. Use the **CDJs** again. Now you play loud enough that Lou bangs on the pipe.
10. Pick up the **milk crate**. You take your three records: Everything But The Girl, Phuture's "Acid Tracks", and your mom's
    Grover Washington Jr. "Winelight".
11. Give **"Winelight"** to the **hole in the wall**. Lou loves Bill Withers and pays you back with a white label marked
    "NYC 93". Keep it safe.
12. Use the **phone** and choose **Call Dante**. This starts a beat.Match audition over the phone.
    **How to win:** use your ears. Two kicks a hair apart sound like a horse galloping, and one clean thump means you
    are locked. Up and down change pitch (Shift for coarse), left and right nudge. Then bring the second deck in with
    the crossfader. Dante books you for Saturday: a vinyl hour, then tracks on a USB.
13. Use **"Acid Tracks" (warped)** on **bed**, then Use the **bed** to sleep. The mattress will flatten it overnight.


## Wednesday morning: the street

14. Pull the **bed** to take the now-flat "Acid Tracks" back.
15. Use the **door**, then Talk to **door 4B (Lou)**. His set tip: start soft, give them something to chew on, and keep
    the good stuff until they have earned it.
16. Use the **stairs down**. On the stoop, **Smitty** offers you $20 and a slice for dropping a bag at Sal's. Pick
    "Twenty? I'm in." and then "Fine. Hand it over." (Use the package to peek if you like. It is oregano.)

## The bodega, Sal's and cash

17. Use the **bodega door**.
18. Use **bag of cans** on the **can-deposit machine**. You get $2.40 (your total is $7.40).
19. **Optional:** Look at **Ali** and pet the **bodega cat**. Her name is Cheese and she is the manager.
20. Use the **door to the street**, then Use **Sal's door**.
21. Talk to Sal if you like (pick "Of course not" when he asks if you peeked). Give **Smitty's package** to **Sal**.
    You get $20 and a **Sal's slice**. Save the slice. You need it for Saturday.
22. Leave and Use the **subway stairs (L train)**. Pick **Synergex Solutions** on the map. Each ride costs $3.

## Synergex: the fire-alarm USB

You want a USB stick from the supply closet, and the closet only opens when the fire alarm goes off.

23. Talk to **Brenda**. Ask "Is there a USB drive I could borrow?" and "What happens if there's a fire?", then back out.
24. Look at the **supply closet**. A sign reads MAGLOCK RELEASES ON FIRE ALARM (CODE 3.1).
25. Talk to **Greg** and **Dev**. Linda keeps her popcorn in the break room, and its smoke detector is very sensitive.
26. Use **break room**.
27. Pick up the **cupboards** to take **Linda's popcorn**.
28. Use the **microwave** (the popcorn goes in). Choose **Press POPCORN**, then choose **Press POPCORN again**.
    The bag says press once. Ignore the bag.
29. The smoke detector goes off and the floor evacuates. You stay behind. Open the **supply closet** and take the
    **USB 3-pack**.

## Deep Cuts: the closer

30. Use the **subway stairs**, pick **Deep Cuts Records** on the map.
31. Talk to **Ray**. He tells you a vinyl hour needs four records and to dig the dollar bins.
32. Use the **dollar bins** to start go.Digging.
    **How to win:** a closer is a big, warm, sing-along record. You cannot tell by the sleeve, so LISTEN and the tag shows.
    Ten dollars is the cap. Buy "Finally" by CeCe Peniston.
33. Ray hands you a **Nowhere flyer** with your name misspelled. Keep it for Saturday.

## Home: load the tracks and sleep

34. Travel **Home (Jefferson St)**. Use the **front door**, then the **door 4C**.
35. Use **USB 3-pack** to open it and keep a stick.
36. Use **laptop** with **USB stick**. Choose **A little of everything (the safe one)**. You get four reliable tracks.
37. Use the **bed**. You sleep through Thursday and Friday (the landlord leaves a note that says "ha") and wake up on
    Saturday night.

## Saturday: Nowhere

38. Leave the apartment, Use the **stairs down**, and take the **subway** to **Nowhere**.
39. Talk to **Tank** on the door. He wants proof and food.
40. Give **Nowhere flyer** to **Tank**.
41. Give **Sal's slice** to **Tank**. He eats it with his eyes closed and lets you in.
42. Use **the door to Nowhere**.
43. Talk to **Dante**, then Talk to **Dani** behind the bar. She will dance if you earn it.
44. Use the **DJ booth** to start crowd.Read.
    **How to win:** the room is nearly empty at ten, so start soft and build. If the bartenders dance, the floor follows.
    Save the white label for when the room has warmed up, and play the closer last.
45. The bartenders dance, the floor fills, and Dante promises to pay you next week. Level 1 is complete.

# Level 2: Underground Hustler

> **Spoilers for Level 2 below.**

**Setup.** Two weeks later you are sleeping on Marcus's couch in Bed-Stuy because the landlord changed the lock on 4C.
You need cash, a shower, turntables and a gig. Vic runs the vinyl party Basement Tapes and gives slots to nobody. You
will fix a dead PA at a loft party, then throw an after-party in an illegal basement.

The first half is **open**: **get clean**, **raise money** and **get into 4C** can go in nearly any order. From Friday
night it is a line.

## Marcus's couch

1. Look at the **window**, **my gear** and the **fridge**. The fridge has a LifeDrop plasma flyer and a Post-it
   about someone called Linda.
2. Talk to **Marcus** and ask everything: "So what's the plan?", "Can I use your shower?", "Tell me about Vic.", and
   "What should I do next?". He texts Dani for you and unlocks three map spots: the plasma center, Eddie's van and
   Dani's place.

## Goal: get clean (Toby's way)

Toby has been in the bathroom since Sunday doing a twelve-step ritual. His only child is Linda, a kombucha culture.

3. Use the **bathroom door** three times. The third knock is for emergencies.
4. Open the **fridge**, then Pick up the **fridge** to take the jar marked "Linda".
5. Use **bathroom door** with **Linda (jar)**. Toby panics, gives you the bathroom and, as thanks, a **lavender sachet**
   to give to Fitz later.

**Alternative: Dani's shower.** See the end of this level.

## Goal: raise money (plasma)

6. Use the **door**, then Talk to **Mrs. Pearl** on the stoop. Ask "How does the plasma thing work?" and "I got locked
   out. What counts as proof of address?". She says a bill, a lease, or an envelope with your name.
7. Use the **subway stairs** and pick **My old building (4C)**.
8. Look at the **notice**, then Use **my door (4C)**. It is locked. Talk to **Lou's door (4B)**.
   Ask for your mail, which gives you a **Con Ed bill (4C)** as proof of address. Ask about "NYC 93" too: Lou admits it
   is his own track. Also ask who painted the hall window shut.
9. Use the **window**. It is painted shut, but there is a seam. You will need something thin and flat later.
10. Use the **stairs down** and pick the **LifeDrop Plasma Center**.
11. Use the **pen on a chain**. It tells you that you need a form first. Talk to **Rhonda** and pick "I'd like to donate
    plasma." She hands you **Form 27-B (blank)**.
12. Use **pen on a chain** with **Form 27-B (blank)**. You fill in the form.
13. Give **Form 27-B (filled out)** to **Rhonda**. She takes the bill and clears you.
14. Talk to **Nurse Bev** and pick "Look at the ceiling". You get a **LifeDrop card ($100)**.
15. Try the **exit**. You are woozy and cannot leave. Use the **aftercare table** for the cookie and the juice.
16. Use the **exit** and travel to **Marcus's block**.
17. Use **LifeDrop card** on the **ATM**. Choose "Withdraw everything ($90)". The machine charges a $9.99 fee.

## Goal: get into 4C (the fire escape)

18. Take the subway to **Eddie's van**. Talk to **Eddie Two-Times** and ask about the AT-LP120-USBs (the sticker is
    $215, the haggled deal about $180), his records ("Gypsy Woman" is $3) and Vic. You cannot afford the turntables yet.
19. Pick up the **bargain blanket** and choose "Take the putty knife ($4)". This is the thing that cracks the seam.
20. Travel to **My old building (4C)**. Use **window** with **putty knife**. You climb the fire escape into your own
    kitchen window.
21. Inside 4C you take $35 from under Gerald's saucer and the **demo cassette** ("BED-STUY MIX VOL. 2"). Keep the
    cassette for Vic.
22. Use **stairs down**, travel to **Marcus's block**, and Use **Marcus's door**.

## Friday: Marcus's birthday party

23. Use the **couch** and choose "Sleep until Friday (Marcus's party)".
24. Use **the door (guest list)** and pick "Stick to the list, with a tiny bit of mercy". The party fills, and
    Marcus's cover jar pays you $108.
25. Give **cat-photo USB stick** to **Jess**. She lends you her spare power amp for the loft. Twinkie gives you "Show
    Me Love" and hints at Vic and an after-party.

## Saturday: turntables

26. Use the **door**, travel to **Eddie's van**, and Talk to **Eddie**. Pick "I want the AT-LP120-USB pair." This starts
    **buy.Gear**.
    **How to win:** inspect and test each listing, haggle, and spot the broken one before you pay ("it was working this
    morning" is a warning). A good pair costs about $175, and Eddie throws in Inner City's "Good Life".

## The loft on Meserole

27. Travel to **The loft on Meserole**. Give **lavender sachet** to **Fitz** on the door. He lets you in.
28. Talk to **Moxie** and pick "I've got a spare amp." She points you at the rack.
29. Talk to **Kosmo Delacroix** and pick "Any advice, Mr. Delacroix?". He gets your name wrong, but the advice is real:
    the hum is always the ground, so follow the cable nobody loves.
30. Use **borrowed power amp** on **amp rack**.
31. Use the **DJ booth** to start sound.Check.
    **How to win:** trace the chain: decks, mixer, DI, EQ, amp, speakers. UNMUTE the PA, play a deck, then listen. Pink
    noise tests the amp and speakers alone. A hum means try the ground lift. Fix every fault, then open the doors.
32. Moxie pays you out of the door money (+$40). Vic watches from the back and the Basement Tapes spot appears on your map.

## Basement Tapes

33. Use **door (outside)**, take **subway stairs**, and travel to **Basement Tapes** (Gowanus).
34. Talk to **Twinkie** and start after.Party.
    **How to win:** before sunrise you need people, a location and a sound system. The location is sorted, so text your
    contacts (Marcus, Dani, Moxie) and watch your phone battery.
35. Use **turntables (boxed pair)** on the **DJ booth**. Your set ends with Lou's "NYC 93".
36. Vic walks in. Keep the **demo cassette** ready. It gets handed over. Then promoter.Talk starts.
    **How to win:** a dialogue duel with patience and interest meters. Do not sound desperate and never say "please" to
    Vic. Show you are not in a rush, that you know his party, and mention the white label.
37. Vic gives you a monthly slot (last Saturday, 11 PM to 12:30), cash on the night, and a copy of Mr. Fingers'
    "Can You Feel It". Level 2 is complete.

## Alternatives for Level 2

- **Alternative: Dani instead of Toby.** Once Marcus has texted her, travel to **Dani's place** (Greenpoint) and Talk to
  her. Pick "I'll be honest, I really just need a shower." You get clean, a name to drop at the loft door, and you can
  ask "Could I take some pierogi to go?" (or Pick up the **coffee table**).
- **Alternative: Kowalski and the key to 4C.** Tuesday 2 PM is the building manager's office hour. In the hall, Talk to
  **Mr. Kowalski** and ask for the key to 4C. He will not budge for nothing: Give **Dani's pierogi** to him and pay $40.
  You get the key and he puts in a word with Vic. Use **my door (4C)** with **key to 4C**.
- **Alternative: Rosa's side job.** At **Rosa's Deli Grocery** you can earn $18 a run, twice per chapter.
- **Alternative: sneaking into the loft.** Without the sachet, Push the **dumpster**, then Use the **dangling ladder**.
- **Alternative: a lemon at Eddie's.** If you miss the broken deck in buy.Gear, the sale does not go through. Try again, or settle for a cheaper deck.
- **Alternative: mini-game failures.** You can fail sound.Check or after.Party once and retry. If promoter.Talk goes
  badly three times, Vic gives in anyway.

# Level 3: Club DJ

> **Spoilers for Level 3 below.**

**Setup.** Six months later you have a Thursday residency at the Halcyon Room. Odessa has three house rules: be early,
stay sober, bring Gus a snack. Next Thursday is the six-month anniversary, and Brandon Shine, who plays Saturdays, is
acting very friendly. Odessa's lipstick napkin lists what to do first: decks, a mixer, a grail record and a promo week.

Night 1 and the final night are straight lines. The week between is **open**: do the gear, grail and promo goals in any order.

## Thursday afternoon: pack the bag

1. Talk to the **hole in the wall**. Lou says to play his white label the night it counts.
2. Use the **door**. It refuses until you have a bag and a USB.
3. Pick up **tracks USB** and Pick up the **record crate** (you get the record bag).
4. Look at the **desk drawer** and Pick up the **desk drawer** to take the **old USB** (the cat photos). Keep this stick.
   It becomes a decoy later.
5. Use the **door** and travel to **The Halcyon Room**.

## Night 1: the street and Gus

6. Talk to **Eddie Two-Times**. He says come back when you are paid.
7. Use **Sunrise Deli**. The dollar menu has pork rinds, salt and vinegar chips and a pickle. Buy the **chips**. Gus
   hates pork rinds, so skip them (or buy them to see him say "No."). Each snack is $2.
8. Use the **Halcyon Room door**. Odessa greets you. Talk to **Nico** and **Bex**, the feuding bartenders.
9. Use the **DJ booth**. The monitors are asleep. Pick up the **gold smiley sticker** (Brandon's signature), then Use the
   **steps to the floor**.
10. Talk to **Gus**. He says "Snack." Give **salt & vinegar chips** to **Gus**. He wakes the monitors with a clap test.
11. Use the **DJ booth** and then Use the **CDJs**. This starts crowd.Read.
    **How to win:** the bar is empty at ten PM, so start soft, build, and let the room find you. It fills like a tub.
12. Odessa pays you **$200** and hands you a lipstick napkin. You wake up Friday at 1:20 PM with **$316**.

## The week (any order)

Use the **bed** to sleep only when the napkin is done. The bed refuses until the open goals are finished. You have
until Thursday, and this is the one stretch where money matters.

### Goal: decks and a mixer

13. Travel to **The Halcyon Room** (street). Use **Sunrise Deli** and buy the **pickle** for $2. Gus wants it.
14. Talk to **Eddie** and pick the **Pioneer CDJ-900** option (you can haggle first). This starts buy.Gear.
    **How to win:** inspect before you buy and do not touch the listing with the sticker. A good deal in the main
    walkthrough costs about $190.
15. Use the **Halcyon Room door**. In daylight Odessa is mopping.
16. Give **bodega pickle** to **Gus**. He gives you a dusty **Pioneer DJM-350** with no power brick.
17. Use the **door to Ludlow Street** and Talk to **Eddie**. Choose "Buy the power brick ($35)".

### Goal: the grail record (a three-stop chain)

18. Travel to **Deep Cuts**. Look at the **'NOT FOR SALE' wall**. Talk to **Ray** and pick "I heard you had something for me."
    Ray tells you about Loose Joints, "Is It All Over My Face?" and that **Mort at the Sunday record fair** knows the
    collector. A new map spot appears.
19. Travel to the **Sunday Record Fair**. Talk to **Mort**. He grunts and wants a coffee.
20. **Optional:** Talk to **Priya**, a rival digger, and to **Danny Krivit**, a friendly cameo.
21. Look at the **corkboard** and Pick up the **corkboard**. You take the **estate-sale flyer**. The small print under the
    address is important.
22. Talk to the **PTA lady** and choose **Black** ($5). Black coffee is what Mort wants.
23. Give **PTA coffee** to **Mort**. He tells you about Hal Pruitt, a collector in Woodside whose niece is emptying the
    house this week. A map spot appears.
24. Travel to **The Pruitt house**. Priya is already bidding $110 for the whole house. Talk to **Tanya**.
25. First choose "Let me look around first." Then Talk to Tanya again and choose **Show her the estate-sale flyer**. The
    small print says no dealers. You buy the whole house for **$70** and Priya leaves.
26. Use each of the three **shelves** and choose **Keep the starred sleeves**. Hal put a pencil star on every sleeve that
    made him cry. That keeps eight records, and a dealer outside pays for the leftovers.
27. Pick up the **Technics turntables**. The dust sheet will not lie flat. Talk to **Bernard (parakeet)** until he says
    "LEFT PLATTER".
28. Pick up the **record on the platter**. This is the Loose Joints. Then Pick up the **Technics turntables**.

### Goal: the promo week

29. Travel to **Apartment 4C**. Use the **laptop** and choose "Start the posting week". Pick **Ten-second clips from the
    booth, one a day, no talking**. You gain 320 fans.
30. Use the **bed** to sleep. It is now Thursday.

## Thursday: anniversary night

31. **Important:** Use **record bag** with **tracks USB**. The real stick goes into a Sylvester sleeve in the bag. Your
    old cat USB stays in your jacket pocket as bait. Brandon is a hugger, and hugging is how he steals.
32. Travel to **The Halcyon Room**. Talk to **Twinkie** (a hint about the hug), **Marcus** and look at the queue. Marcus
    arrives as "plus three" with Dee, Rico and the guy from the cab. **Tiny** lets them all in.
33. Use the **Halcyon Room door**. Brandon hugs you and steals the cat stick. Choose "Let him come". You smile.
34. Talk to **Marcus**, **Brandon** and **Kosmo Delacroix**.
35. Use the **DJ booth**. Kosmo is playing until midnight. Your real USB is safe.
36. Use the **steps to the floor**. Brandon plugs your fake stick into the LED wall and a hundred people see Mr.
    Whiskers forty feet wide. Odessa sends Brandon to her office.
37. Talk to **Odessa**. She tells you to be ready.
38. Use the **DJ booth** and then Use the **CDJs**. Kosmo overruns. This starts crowd.Read.
    **How to win:** the room is warm but wobbling. Do not cool it and do not spike it.
39. Use the **CDJs** again for the anniversary set. Pick the **first record** (Everything But The Girl, "Missing"),
    the **last record** (CeCe Peniston, "Finally"), and when the Loose Joints comes out: **at the peak, after the
    white label, with the room already up**.
40. Odessa gives you Saturday's main floor, the old **Dynaudio BM5 mkIII** monitors, and your $200.

## The way home

41. At the Delancey platform your MetroCard has expired. Choose **Ask the man with the oranges for a swipe**. Marcus
    told you about him.
42. Home at 3 AM, Lou asks if you played his 1993. You did. The booking agent's email from Berlin is waiting on your
    phone. Level 3 is complete.

## Money tips for Level 3

- You start the week with **$316**. A good CDJ deal ($190), the brick ($35), coffee ($5), pickle ($2) and the flyer
  price ($70) all fit.
- If you run short, Odessa offers a weekday shift for **$55** (up to two), and Mort buys records at 60 cents on the dollar.
- On anniversary night Marcus's plus three run up a $200 bar tab, so you leave with $142. That is rent money.

## Alternatives for Level 3

- **Alternative: the fallback when you forget to hide the USB.** If you do not hide the real stick, Brandon swaps it
  at the hug and your drive is full of Brandon. You can still win it back. Odessa wants proof. Her bar-cam DVR needs two
  keys, held by Nico and Bex, who have not spoken since March. Talk to **Nico** and
  **Bex** and ask "What do you listen to when you're not angry?" (both secretly love Sylvester). Use **record bag** on
  **bar record player** and choose **Sylvester**. They make up and give you their keys. Use the **staff door**, then Use the **bar-cam DVR** in Odessa's office to get the
  **bar-cam clip**. Give the clip to **Odessa**. When Brandon is confronted, the cleaner choice is to say nothing and let
  the clip run again. Raising your voice costs a $40 fine and some fans. Talking to Brandon directly lets you keep it
  quiet in exchange for your USB, at the price of an extra $60 on the bar tab.
- **Alternative: a tight budget.** One run paid $260 for the decks, worked two weekday shifts for Odessa, sold two
  records to Mort, skipped the corkboard flyer and told Tanya about Hal's reputation ("Mort and Ray") for a $90 price,
  kept six of the eight stars, took a $40 fine for tagging every bar in the promo week, and jumped the turnstile
  at the platform. It still finished the level.

# Level 4: Touring DJ

> **Spoilers for Level 4 below.**

**Setup.** A booking agent named Ingrid Falk wants you at Halle Neun in Berlin for a 2 AM set. You have $80, a heavy bag
of records and an old white label to remix. You will release a record with Lou, negotiate a fee and a rider, then survive
JFK, a lost bag, a ticket machine, a strict doorman and a hotel where Axl Vanta has stolen your room.

The level is a straight line, with money tight at JFK and in Berlin.

## The studio (Thursday)

1. Use the **laptop**. You read Ingrid's email. She wants a release.
2. Pick up **Gerald** (your passport is under his leaves).
3. Pick up the **box from Ray**. It holds six techno and minimal records and a note: Berlin people respect records, so
   read the sleeves.
4. Talk to the **hole**. Lou comes over. Talk to **Lou** and ask "What's the story behind NYC 93?" and "Can I put it out?
   On my own label?" He wants fifty-fifty and his name on it, spelled right. Agree.
5. Use **Technics** with **white label "NYC 93"**.
6. Use the **sampler** to start mak.Tracks.
   **How to win:** a 16-step sequencer. Fill the kick, clap, hat and bass rows around the chopped sample and make the
   drop hit. Lou is watching: do not chop his hi-hat. Fail three times and the game lets you through.
7. Use **phone** on the **hole** to take a cover photo of Lou's hole in the wall. This is your label, Pipe Hole Records.
8. Use the **laptop** to release the track. Pick a title and hand the socials to Marcus ("plus three") for the promo
    week. A video of Lou's cat Biscuit falling off a couch goes viral.
9. Ingrid calls. This starts promoter.Talk.
    **How to win:** negotiate fee, flights, hotel and rider. **Refuse exposure** every time it is offered. A good deal
    is $1,800 plus economy flights, a hotel and a rider: water, a towel, and a vegetable that is not a pickle.
10. Pick up the **record bag** (you cannot leave without it). Use the **door** and travel to **JFK Airport, Terminal 4**.
    The cab costs $8.

## JFK: the bag and the $75 pinch

You arrive with $72 and the airline wants $75 for an overweight bag. Fix that first.

11. Give **passport** to **Dolores (check-in)**. You get a boarding pass for seat 23A.
12. Give **record bag** to **Dolores**. The fee is $75 and you cannot pay.
13. Talk to the **man in a Hawaiian shirt** (Gil). Pick "I could sell you a record. ($40)" and sell one you can spare
    (the $3 dollar-bin "Gypsy Woman" fetches $40). Now you can pay.
14. Give **record bag** to **Dolores** again. You get a claim tag, then a PA call: security has detained your bag.
15. Pick up the **forms rack** to take **Form 7B** (it only works after the airline calls you in).
16. Use the **snack machine**. Buy the **beef jerky** ($4, bribery for later) and a **bottle of water** ($3, the first
    rider item). Skip the pickle in a pouch: it is the wrong vegetable.
17. Use **Baggage Services**.

## Baggage Services and Security

18. Talk to **Bernadette**. Ask about the detained bag, "What are you watching?", "That's my remix. I made it.", then
    show her the Pipe Hole Records page on your phone. She screams and stamps your **Form 7B**. She adds a smiley.
19. Use the **Terminal** door, then Use **Security & Gates**.
20. Give **Form 7B (stamped)** to **Officer Delgado**. He signs it.
21. Give **beef jerky** to **Nugget** the beagle. Delgado stares at the wall. Nugget gets off your bag.
22. Pick up the **inspection table** to get your bag back.
23. Use **Security & Gates** again to reach the plane.

## The plane

24. Use **seat 23A**. You cannot sit with the bag on your lap.
25. Talk to **Marguerite (flight attendant)**. Use the **cart** twice. Take a **towel** (free) and a **tomato juice**
    ($4) for its celery stick. That covers the second and third rider items.
26. Talk to **Kosmo Delacroix**. He calls you Brenton. Give **record bag** to **Kosmo** and he lets it sit on his spare
    seat, gently.
27. Use **seat 23A** and choose "Sit down." (If you picked "Not yet" you can try again.) The flight takes a long time.

**Alternative:** if you are broke, tell Kosmo a joke instead. A good one earns $60.

## Berlin: the last train

You land at 11:52 PM and the last airport train leaves at 12:07.

28. Look at the **network map**. You are in zone C. Pick up the **platform bin** to take eight one-way plastic bottles.
    You can redeem them later.
29. Use the **ticket machine**. Buy **Zones A, B and C ($4.90)**. A ticket without C is the wrong ticket. If you buy
    A and B by mistake, use the machine again and choose **Upgrade to zones A, B and C ($2.00)**.
30. Use the **ticket validator**. A ticket you do not stamp is just paper.
31. Use the **airport express**. Choose **Change at Ostkreuz, like the map says**. The other route is the bad choice and
    ends with a taxi, late and skint.

## The street, the Späti and Sven

32. Pick up the **empty bottles** on the curb. Talk to **Kemal** at the Späti. Ask "Tell me about Sven.", then pick "I have
    some empties for the Pfand." You get $2.48. Ask "What do you sell?" and buy a **Club-Mate ($2)**. Nobody ever
    brings the doorman a drink.
33. Use **to Halle Neun**.
34. Give **Club-Mate** to **Sven**. That answers question one for you.
35. Talk to **Sven**. He asks about the labels of your records. A wrong answer just repeats the question.
    **Quiz tips:** Robert Hood, "Minimal Nation" is on **Axis**. Ben Klock, "One" is on **Ostgut Ton**. The record
    facts are in the **record crate** in your phone menu. Read them on the train.
36. You get a **Halle Neun wristband**. Use the **door**.

## Halle Neun: the set

37. Talk to **Jeff Mills** for a nod.
38. Give **bottle of water** to **Katja**. Give **towel** to **Katja**. She ticks them off. The vegetable goes over at the
    till after your set.
39. Use the **DJ booth**. First comes beat.Match, then crowd.Read.
    **How to win:** beat.Match works as before. For crowd.Read, build a techno floor slowly with your Berlin records.
40. At the till Katja counts the vegetable too. A complete rider adds a $100 bonus. You collect $1,900 and 4,200 fans.
    Jeff Mills says "Nice record."

## The hotel

41. Use the **steel door**, then Use **Hotel Zum Pfau**.
42. Use the **elevator**. You have no key. Talk to **Herr Brandt (night clerk)**. Ask what Axl is doing here and whether
    anything makes him get up. The answer: Axl cannot resist a pen.
43. Give **passport** to **Herr Brandt**. This is the important bit. The clerk has to be on your side first.
44. Pick up the **postcard rack** to take a **hotel postcard**.
45. Give **hotel postcard** to **Axl Vanta**. He signs it, the clerk mentions the Kaiserin Suite, and Axl leaves.
46. Pick up the **room key**. Use the **elevator**. You sleep eleven hours, and Ingrid offers you the headline slot at
    Harbor Lights. Level 4 is complete.

**Note:** if Axl gets the postcard before the clerk has seen your passport, he just keeps the key.

## Money tips for Level 4

- You start with $80, and the cab costs $8. The overweight fee is $75, so sell **one** record to Gil for $40 first.
- Buy the water and jerky at JFK and the juice on the plane. Keep cash for the Berlin ticket.
- Keep the plastic bottles. Kemal pays a quarter each (eight cents for glass).
- A complete rider adds $100 to the $1,800 fee. Water, a warm towel, and a vegetable that is not a pickle.

## Alternatives for Level 4

- **Alternative: failing mini-games.** The game still lets you through. A weak promoter.Talk gives a smaller fee, still a deal.
- **Alternative: the unhappy path.** Ride with the wrong ticket and you are fined, then a taxi gets you there late and
  skint. You still play.
- **Alternative: recovering from broke.** At the ticket machine, a stag party will buy a record for $20. At Kemal's Späti
  you can redeem the bottles, busk twice and sell a record, then buy water, a carrot and a dish towel for the rider. A
  carrot counts as a vegetable and a dish towel counts as a towel.
- **Alternative: a failed quiz.** Wrong answers repeat, and Sven lets you in eventually. Each rider item you miss comes off your fee.

# Level 5: Superstar DJ

> **Spoilers for Level 5 below.**

**Setup.** Harbor Lights wants you to headline on Saturday night, and your manager Margo Quist has wired a $4,000
advance. You will buy a rig, do a radio interview, sort a guest list, invite Lou, then spend Saturday fixing your pass,
placating Axl Vanta, soundchecking and taking the room from Axl when he overruns. It ends at sunrise on the pier.

Thursday and Friday are goal lists. On Saturday, once your pass is fixed, the soundcheck and Axl can go in either order.

## Thursday: gear

You start with about $5,500. The Taula, the Core 47 monitors and the CDJ-3000 decks together cost more than you have, so
you must choose.

1. Look around the studio. The **bed** refuses until the Taula is bought.
2. Use the **clothes rail** and choose **The pink-lined coat**. It is black outside and hot pink inside.
3. Take the **subway** to **Lacquer & Wire** (Williamsburg). Eddie Two-Times is there, too.
4. Talk to **Mercè** and pick "I want the Taula." This starts buy.Gear.
   **How to win:** Mercè asks three questions. Do not lie. The Taula costs **$1,650**.
5. Talk to **Eddie** and ask about the **Core 47** monitors. This starts a second buy.Gear in his van. They cost
   **$3,200**.
6. Talk to **Mercè** and ask "What about decks?". The CDJ-3000 pair is $3,200, which you cannot afford. The used
   **CDJ-900NXS** pair is $990.
7. If you are short, Talk to **Eddie** and choose "Eddie, buy some of my records." He pays **$450** each and stops after two
   (Acid Tracks and Hardrive are the usual picks).
8. Choose the used 900NXS pair. The shop asks you to confirm, and you can say "Not yet" first. Then confirm.
9. Travel to **Your studio**. Look at the **delivery boxes** and the **record crate**. Use the **bed** to sleep.

**Alternative: the cheap, honest rig.** Buy only the Taula. Ask Mercè "I'm short" and choose "Call Margo" for a $600
advance on the fee. Keep your old CDJs and monitors. At home the bed asks if you want more shopping. Sleeping on it is fine.

## Friday: the radio, the gate and Lou

10. Travel to **Radio Harbor 88.1**. Look at the **control room window**. Talk to **Dana Okafor**.
11. Pick the first option each time: "One questionable decision at a time.", "The bartenders danced.", "A white label nobody's
    heard. It's my neighbor's." and "No comment. But I'd like it noted: I have a Taula." These score well. Do not say
    Axl Vanta's name on air.
12. Callers cut in (Ray, Marcus, Eddie, Dante). Dante promises on air to pay you, which matters on the pier.
13. When Dana asks for a record, pick **CeCe Peniston, "Finally"**. You gain 6,400 fans.
14. Dana offers a paid read for Eddie's Spirit Water. Choose **Read it (+$1,100)** for the cash (you lose 200 fans), or
    decline.
15. Travel to **Harbor Lights Festival**. Talk to **Marcus**. He says Whit's list comes first.
16. Talk to **Whit**. You get your all-access pass, hand-laminated and spelled wrong.
17. Talk to **Marcus** again and choose **Marcus picks four. Nine on the list, and Whit keeps three comps for the
    walk-ups.** Whit gives you an envelope for Lou.
18. Travel to **Your studio**. Talk to the **hole in the wall** and choose "I want you to come, Lou." He says to slide the
    invitation under the pipe.
19. Give **Lou's pass** to the **hole in the wall**. He agrees, and wants his good navy cardigan.
20. Use the **bed** to sleep until Saturday.

## Saturday: the pass chain

Gloria on the artist gate will not let in a wrong pass. Everything backstage is behind Gloria.

21. Use the **door**. You are at the gate. Talk to **Margo** and **Whit**.
22. Use **to the festival grounds**. Look at your **pass**. Talk to **Gloria**. She refuses and tells you the pass needs
    permanent ink because it is laminated.
23. Look at **Ray's price mug**, then Talk to **Ray**. Choose "Got a Sharpie, Ray?" to get a **Sharpie**. Ask his advice
    too: start low, climb, and don't peak before midnight.
24. Talk to **Twinkie** and ask "What do you know about Axl Vanta?". **Key hint:** call him Alex and mention his mother's
    banner in the front row.
25. **Optional:** Talk to **Jess** and promise Mr. Whiskers a shout-out in the set.
26. Use **Sharpie** on **pass**. You rewrite your name in capital letters.
27. Give **pass** to **Gloria**. She lets you through.
28. Use the **artist gate** to go backstage.

## Saturday: Axl and the soundcheck (either order)

29. **Optional:** Talk to **Louie Vega**, **DJ Spinna** and **Francois K**. They all say "Have a great set."
30. Talk to **Roz**, the stage manager. She explains Axl wants your slot and will not open the door without a smoothie.
31. Pick up the **green smoothie** in catering.
32. Use **Axl's trailer**. Axl is rude and suspicious. Choose the Twinkie line: **"Alex. Your mom's in the front row.
    With a banner."** He folds. You get the 11:45 slot and he promises to be off by 11:30.
33. Use the **stairs to the stage**, then Use the **stage entrance** to reach the main stage.
34. Look at the **LED wall** and the **speaker stacks**. Use the **DJ booth** to start sound.Check.
    **How to win:** Mo (front of house) lists four faults: ground hum, a dead channel, feedback, and a deck patched into
    the wrong input. Unmute the PA, play each deck, listen, and follow the signal chain.
35. When both Axl and the soundcheck are done, night falls. Walk back through the wings to the backstage area. Lou and Marcus
    arrive.

**Alternative: soundcheck first, Axl last.** Do the soundcheck, then fetch the smoothie and go to Axl without Roz's or
Twinkie's help. It works, but Axl's scene goes worse.

## Saturday night: the overrun and the set

36. Talk to **Lou** and **Marcus**. Lou is wearing the navy cardigan.
37. Use the **stairs to the stage**. Axl is an hour into a set that had no encores. Roz asks how you want to take the room.
    Any of the three answers works. The first, **Walk on, take the second channel and slide the room away from him,
    one record at a time**, is the most fun.
38. Talk to **Roz** and **Lou**. Use the **stage entrance** to reach the main stage at night.
39. Use the **DJ booth** to start the headline set. Choose an opener (**Open low: Mr. Fingers, "Can You Feel It".
    Let the field lean in.**) and a closer (**Close it down: Mood II Swing, "Closer".**).
    **How to win crowd.Read:** Ray's rules apply. Start low, climb, do not peak before midnight, do not jump more than
    four BPM unless you mean it, and if the room goes weird, play something they know.
40. Next comes a beat.Match: Lou's white label into CeCe's "Finally". No waveform helps you. Use your ears and mind the
    dead spot in the left pitch fader.
41. Mr. Whiskers gets his shout-out, and Kosmo finally says your real name. You gain 25,000 fans.

## Sunrise on the pier

42. Use **the sunrise** once to learn you are not done. You must talk to people first.
43. Talk to **Lou** (he adopts the cat Biscuit) and **Dante**. Dante pays you in 400 Nowhere drink tickets. It is the best
    and the worst paycheck you have ever had.
44. Talk to **Marcus**, **Ray**, **Twinkie**, **Eddie**, **Jess**, **Margo** and **Kosmo**. Margo's fee clears into your wallet.
45. Use **the sunrise**. Level 5 is complete.

## Money tips for Level 5

- Money is loose, but you cannot afford the Taula, the Core 47s and the CDJ-3000s together. Buy the Taula first.
- The paid radio read is $1,100 for 200 fans. Margo's advance is a $600 loan against your fee, with ten percent on top.
- The fee is $5,000 less $200 for the sunrise invoice, and less the advance if you took it.

## Alternatives for Level 5

- **Alternative: cheap rig.** Taula only, Margo's advance, your old CDJs and monitors, no radio plug.
- **Alternative: the overrun.** Any of the three answers works, including "Wait it out. He promised."

## Appendix: the streaming patch

There is a hidden extra. On the title screen, click the logo: three times on "re:", twice on "parteé", then three more
times on "re:". The screen flashes and the streaming patch turns on: the jukebox, and the records that play during the
story, play the actual record (an official 30-second preview, looping) instead of the built-in loop. It needs an
internet connection; when a record cannot be found, the loop plays as before. A switch for it appears under HOW TO
PLAY once it is unlocked. The mini-games keep their built-in loops either way, so beat.Match plays the same.
