# Instagram kit for re:parteé

Everything here is rendered from the game's own art and fonts by `node marketing/instagram/make.mjs`.

| File | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `story-1.png` | 1080x1920 | Story slide 1: the pitch (bedroom to main stage) |
| `story-2.png` | 1080x1920 | Story slide 2: what is in it (levels, mini-games, records) |
| `story-3.png` | 1080x1920 | Story slide 3: play it now, with both links |
| `post.png` | 1080x1350 | Feed post (4:5) |

The game link is printed on every image. The GitHub link is printed on `story-3.png` and `post.png`.

## Links

- Game: https://repartee-umber.vercel.app
- Link in bio page: https://repartee-umber.vercel.app/links
- Code: https://github.com/CarlosFranzetti/Repartee

## 1. Set the link in bio

Profile, Edit profile, Links, Add external link.

- URL: `https://repartee-umber.vercel.app/links`
- Title: `re:parteé: play the game`

Bio text (pick one; both are under Instagram's 150 characters):

> I made a game. re:parteé: a pixel-art DJ adventure, from bedroom DJ to festival headliner. Free in your browser. Link below.

> re:parteé, a pixel-art point-and-click DJ adventure. 5 levels, 8 mini-games, 105 real records. Play it free. Link below.

## 2. Post the story (three slides)

For each of `story-1.png`, `story-2.png`, `story-3.png`, in that order:

1. New story, pick the image from your camera roll. Do not pinch or zoom it: it is already the full 9:16 frame.
2. Tap the sticker icon, choose **Link**.
   - URL: `https://repartee-umber.vercel.app`
   - Tap "Customize sticker text" and type: `PLAY re:parteé`
3. Drag the sticker onto the pink address bar in the image (bottom of slides 1 and 2, upper part of slide 3), so the
   printed address and the tappable one sit in the same place.
4. On slide 3 only, a second option: use the GitHub address for a second story if you want the code link tappable too.
   Instagram allows one link sticker per story, so the game link goes first.
5. Share to Your story. Then, from your profile, add the three to a Highlight called `re:parteé` so they outlive 24 hours.

The top and bottom strips of each slide are left empty on purpose: Instagram draws your name and the reply bar there.

## 3. Post to the feed

Upload `post.png` (crop set to 4:5, the tall option). Caption:

> I made a game.
>
> re:parteé is a pixel-art point-and-click DJ adventure. You start as a broke bedroom DJ in New York with three records and a mixer that sounds like bacon frying, and you talk, dig and mix your way to a festival main stage. One questionable decision at a time.
>
> 5 levels. 8 mini-games (beatmatching, crate digging, reading a dance floor, fixing a dead PA). 105 real records, each with its story. Every sound is synthesized live in the browser.
>
> It is free, there is nothing to install, and it plays on a phone or a desktop. Link in bio.
>
> The code is on GitHub: CarlosFranzetti/Repartee
>
> #indiegame #pixelart #pointandclick #adventuregame #djlife #housemusic #indiedev #gamedev #webgame #retrogaming #vinyl #cratedigging #javascript #webaudio

Shorter caption, if you prefer:

> I made a game. re:parteé: from broke-ass bedroom DJ to festival headliner, one questionable decision at a time. Free in your browser, link in bio.
>
> #indiegame #pixelart #pointandclick #djlife #housemusic #indiedev

Alt text for the image (Advanced settings, Accessibility):

> The title of a pixel-art game, re:parteé, above a screenshot of a festival main stage at night with a DJ booth and a crowd, and the address repartee-umber.vercel.app.

Links in a caption are not tappable on Instagram, which is why the caption says "link in bio".

## 4. Sharing the link elsewhere

The site now has a preview card (`/og.png`), so pasting https://repartee-umber.vercel.app into WhatsApp, iMessage,
X or Discord shows the title art, the tagline and the address.

Five levels, eight story mini-games and 105 records in the catalog are counts from the game's own data on
2026-11-19. "Free" and "nothing to install" describe the hosted web build.
